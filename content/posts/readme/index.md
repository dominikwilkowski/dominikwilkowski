## Focus
My focus will always be the core business.
I thrive on change but will always work on bringing order to the chaos.
I return to first principles and stay grounded in business realities.
However, my bias is product: creating something that customers love and want to come back for.

## Optimal conditions
Give me a target or a problem and I will be on it.
I like clarity, momentum and getting things moving.
I love collaborating with experts and customers to ensure we're doing the right thing.
I work closely with design to get the best out of software.
I thrive in a positive environment and will go above and beyond in such a place.
While I can (and have) worked in negative environments, you'll get less bang for your buck.
I work hard to create the same environment for my team and the company.
I care deeply about how teams are set up for success, not just what they ship.
Clear ownership, good management and strong feedback loops make a huge difference.
I require honest, real feedback while valuing empathy and tact.
I know I don't know everything and value input and expertise.

## Approachability
With many years of experience, I come with strong opinions but value learning new things every day.
I'm always open to being challenged and taught something new.
I make fun of myself to remain approachable and invite collaboration.
This is my way of creating high-performing teams.
However it's important to me to be able to shift tone as there are times when we have to buckle down and work (although a good dad joke can work wonders with the right timing).

## Leading teams
I work hard to embrace people's strengths but I'll also seek out lost causes and weed out toxicity to prevent wasted effort on either side.
I lead from the front and will get hands-on when needed to unblock teams, raise standards and build momentum all while making sure I don't become the bottle neck for teams or a disrupter their velocity.
Sometimes I teach new languages or concepts as I get excited about new things as much as others.
The best teams are the teams who love solving problems, so I foster curiosity and everyone's inner nerd.
I create a transparent environment and give people the option to know more if they so choose.
The best productivity is created in curious and psychologically safe places.

## Work ethic
- I believe in transparency by default.
- I approach problems as a puzzle to be solved and really enjoy out-of-the-box thinking and breaking with convention where appropriate.
- I believe in under-promising, over-delivering, delighting the user/customer.
- Details/nuance matter greatly to me.
- I'm comfortable moving between strategy and execution, from the details of software to the shape and health of the team building it.
- I like using new tools pragmatically e.g. with AI, I'm interested in what genuinely improves speed, quality and learning, not hype.

## The magic <span role="img" aria-label="A magic wand" tabIndex="0" class="emoji">🪄</span>
I believe the most amazing things can happen when you bridge disciplines that traditionally have been kept apart.
For example the intersection of design/UX and engineering or hardware and software.
I'll always foster an environment where this is as easy and natural as possible to make "handovers" redundant.

## Imperfection
I have [ADHD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) and [ASD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autism), which is a great mix of two forces keeping each other in balance.
Understanding these and their challenges will benefit anyone who works with people like me.
I will work hard and sometimes, when responsibilities and influence are misaligned, I burn out.
I'm always available for people but this is also a risk in overloading me.
My signs of burnout are usually high working hours and little sleep.
